RTIS 生成實作台 Breeding bench

親手走完一次生成：選親代、配對部位、決定每個基因來自誰、突變，然後看身體長出來。Run one generation by hand: pick parents, match parts, choose each gene's source, mutate, and watch the body grow.

1

選親代 Pick parents

收進名冊的子代也會出現在這裡。Offspring you keep show up here too.

2

部位配對 Match parts

用標籤找出兩邊對應的部位，分數越高越像。Tags decide which parts correspond. A higher score means a closer match.

3

決定基因 Choose genes

每一格是一個基因，整個來自 A 或 B。點一下換邊，模型會跟著變。Each square is one gene, taken whole from A or B. Click to switch and the model updates.

4

突變 Mutate

每次擲骰會改 0 到 4 個基因。Each roll changes 0 to 4 genes.
